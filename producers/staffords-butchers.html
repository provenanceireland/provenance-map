Staffords Butchers

Enniscorthy, Co. Wexford

Hand Reared Beef

Stafford's Butchers has been established in Enniscorthy since 1971. Fifty-five years on Killian runs the counter while Brendan looks after the farm, hand rearing the cattle that supply the shop from twenty minutes up the road in the foothills of the Blackstairs Mountains.

Origin

Where exactly does this come from?

The beef comes from An Gleann Glas, The Green Glen in Kilanne, Co. Wexford. The Stafford family farm sits in the foothills of the Black Stairs Mountains, supplying the butchers counter for four decades.

What are the farming practices?

Every animal is hand reared spending most of the year eating grass in the fields on a 3 day rotation. Wheat, barley and beans are sourced from the local area, and silage is produced on their own farm for the winter. Even the straw used for bedding is sourced locally. Farmyard manure and lime is the main fertiliser used for the grass fields, along with small amounts of fertiliser.

How do they process the meat?

All the animals are slaughtered 1 hour away from the farm before being transported to Stafford's Family Butchers. The sides of beef are hung for 21 days before it reaches the counter. That dry ageing process concentrates flavour and tenderises the meat in a way that industrial processing, which prioritises speed and volume, does not allow for. Every cut is butchered in-house by hand. Nothing is outsourced. Then it is served fresh at the counter.

Why does this meet the Provenance standard?

Stafford's Butchers rears cattle on its own farm and sells direct from their own counter. The farm is visible, with named breeds and processed in house. There is no layer of anonymity between the animal on the Black Stairs foothills and the meat on your plate. That is the Provenance standard.

The Butchers

Brendan & Killian Stafford

Brendan started in the trade in 1961, aged sixteen, on Moore Street in Dublin. He learned it properly before coming home to Wexford and opening his own shop in Enniscorthy ten years later.

He has always gone for traditional breeds. Hereford and Angus. Not the big continental animals that most of the trade moved toward. The cattle are hand reared on the Stafford family farm in Glenglass (An Gleann Glas — The Green Glen), Kilanne, twenty minutes from the counter, in the foothills of the Blackstairs Mountains.

Killian now runs the business and Brendan looks after the farm.
