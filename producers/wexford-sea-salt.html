Wexford Sea Salt

Ballyvaldon, Blackwater, Co. Wexford

Hand Harvested Irish Sea Salt

Nestled along the pristine coastline of Wexford, their sea salt is harvested using traditional methods that honour the purity of the ocean and the heritage of their community. Hand-harvested and unrefined, dried naturally under the sun and wind, preserving the ocean's moisture and essential trace elements.

Origin

Where exactly does this come from?

The Irish Sea along the Wexford coastline in Ireland's sunny southeast. Hand-collected seawater is transported to the production site where filtered seawater flows through a sequence of evaporative ponds. Salt brines crystallise in the final ponds before the harvested salt is dried under the sun and packed for delivery. One coastline. One source. Nothing else.

How is it made or harvested?

The process begins with hand-collected seawater transported to the production site. Filtered seawater flows through a sequence of evaporative ponds. Salt brines crystallise in the final ponds. The salt is then dried naturally under the sun and wind of the Wexford coast, preserving the ocean's moisture and essential trace elements. No artificial drying. No heat processing. 1,008 hours of sunshine in every crystal.

What is in it?

Pure Irish sea salt. Unrefined. Naturally lower in sodium. No washing, no bleaching, no post-harvest treatments and no anti-caking agents. One ingredient. The Irish Sea. Nothing added. Nothing taken away.

Why does this meet the Provenance standard?

Wexford Sea Salt uses 100% off-grid renewable energy sources, such as wind and solar power. Every batch undergoes rigorous screening and analysis to meet stringent food quality and safety standards set by the Environmental Health Office. The production is named, the coastline is named, the process is documented and the founders are real people whose story is told here. Recommended by McKenna Guides, whose support celebrates Ireland's most passionate food producers, recognising their commitment to traditional methods, local sourcing and the unique coastal character of Wexford Sea Salt.

The Founders

Jack & Kevin N'Guinan

Jack, with his culinary passion, inherited a love of natural ingredients from his mother's and grandmother's kitchen in Poland. Fostered in the Polish countryside and enhanced on the Wexford coastline, Jack had always been captivated by the flavours and nuances that sea salt could bring to his dishes.

Kevin, a Wexford-born Architectural Technician with an eye for design, saw the potential to build unique evaporation ponds drawing on traditional sea salt marshes found in the warmer climates of France and Spain, while keeping in mind the Irish weather.

Together Jack and Kevin embarked on their Wexford adventure, diving into the coastal rhythms and gaining deep insights into the land-sea connection. They mastered the time-honoured salt-making process, from collecting pristine seawater to patient evaporation, yielding the purest sea salt crystals.
