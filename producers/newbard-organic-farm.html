Newbard Organic Farm

Lismore, Co. Waterford

Organic Pork, Bacon, Lamb & Chicken

Newbard Organic Farm has been raising heritage breed pigs, lamb, and chicken on certified organic pasture since 2012. Siobhán Hubbard and Ross Newcombe farm on the principle that the soil, the animal, and the person eating it are all part of the same system, and that each deserves to be treated as such.

The Visit

Origin

Where is the farm?

Newbard Farm sits in the beautiful countryside outside Lismore in Co. Waterford, less than 30km from Dungarvan. Siobhán and Ross have farmed this land since 2012, starting with a small group of pigs and expanding gradually into a certified organic operation raising heritage breed pigs, lamb, chicken, and seasonal crops including pumpkins, kale, peas, and oats.

What are the farming practices?

All animals are raised outdoors on pasture that is never sprayed with herbicides or insecticides. They receive a special blend of organic feed from Irish Organic Feeds produced in Cork. Gut health is managed through diverse pasture and selected anthelmintic plants rather than routine chemical worming. Pumpkins, kale, peas, and oats are grown on the farm, and fed directly to the animals. No routine antibiotics are used.

What do they produce?

Pork from heritage breed Oxford Sandy & Black x Tamworth and Berkshire pigs raised outdoors on certified organic pasture. Lamb and chicken raised on the same farm to the same standard. No hormones. Third-party certified organic.

Why does this meet the Provenance standard?

Siobhán and Ross are exactly the type of farmers Provenance was founded to highlight. They are honest, hardworking Irish people with a passion for producing real, healthy, organic, non-toxic food for their community. The farm is visible. The farmers are proud to show their produce. The animals are shown. The practices are certified. That is the Provenance standard.

The Farmers

Siobhán Hubbard & Ross Newcombe

Siobhán and Ross began farming at Cooladalane Lower in 2012, starting with a small group of pigs and, in their own words, "a lot of questions, and a determination to farm in a way that felt right." Over the years that followed, those questions produced answers: certified organic status, heritage breeds chosen for outdoor hardiness and flavour, and a farm that grows its own feed crops and closes the loop with local businesses.

Ross brings years of farming knowledge and a genuine passion for organic, regenerative produce to everything he does at Newbard Farm. That expertise shows in the daily work, tending the animals, managing the land, and keeping the farm running the way it should.

Siobhán is a member of Talamh Beo, the Land Workers' Alliance of Ireland, and is leading a national effort to restore small-scale, farmer-led abattoirs across Ireland. The closure of the local abattoir that had served small producers in the south east left farms like Newbard with longer transport distances, higher costs, and greater animal stress at the point of slaughter. Rather than absorb the problem, she began organising a farmer-led alternative, securing Department of Agriculture funding, conducting HACCP research, and working with more than 160 farmers across the country who are ready to process locally as soon as conditions allow.

"We need to bring back the local producers because so many people have lost the connection with food."
— Siobhán Hubbard
