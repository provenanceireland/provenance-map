Provenance Founder

Gorse Farm

Bunclody, Wexford

Certified Organic Market Garden

A certified organic market garden run by Jenny Watkins and Janet Power, two nature enthusiasts with a passion for healthy natural foods. On half an acre of south-facing growing space in the foothills of Mount Leinster, they grow organic leafy greens and vegetables.

The Visit

Origin

Where exactly does this come from?

Gorse Farm is located in Bunclody on the border between the counties of Carlow and Wexford, in a scenic spot looking out at Mount Leinster and the Blackstairs mountain range. The specific growing beds sit on half an acre of south-facing land. The farm is three acres in total, with the remaining land dedicated to nature.

How is it grown?

Farming at Gorse Farm is based around nurturing the soil and protecting the environment. Growing with nature ensures that their soil is rich in minerals, which in turn ensures that crops are both high in nutrients and delicious. Even with limited space they give 25% of their land to nature through developing new hedgerows, shelter belts, flowers and allowing rewilding. Every crop is harvested by hand and packed for local distribution.

What is in it?

Produce is free from chemicals and pesticides. Salad bags contain up to twelve different varieties of leaves depending on the season — from lettuces and mild Chinese mustard leaves to rocket, spinach, kale and edible flowers. One ingredient. Named soil. Named growers. Nothing added.

Why does this meet the Provenance standard?

The vegetable garden received full organic certification in February 2018. Janet and Jenny's commitment to growing food for their community that is not just certified organic but nutrient dense and grown in a way that protects and enhances nature on their holding is an inspiration. They share their passion with their community through walks, talks and engagement — building strong connections between their farm, their customers and nature. Janet is a former board member of the Irish Organic Association and a passionate advocate of small-scale food production, having spoken at Talamh Beo forums on local food systems.

The Growers

Janet Power & Jenny Watkins

Janet is a passionate advocate of agroecology and local food and an active member of Talamh Beo — Ireland's farmers and citizens organisation working for agroecology, regenerative farming and food sovereignty. Talamh Beo are Ireland's only members of the European Coordination Via Campesina — itself part of La Via Campesina, the international movement of farmers with more than 180 member organisations in 82 countries.

Janet believes that people in Ireland want to eat Irish grown produce. The demand is there but there isn't enough supply. Gorse Farm is one of the farms proving that small-scale organic production is commercially viable, ecologically sound and worth protecting.
