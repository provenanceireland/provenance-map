How it works

Each farm tells Provenance every week what they have available. Press See the farm for that farm's list, along with the date they last sent it, so you can see for yourself how current it is.

Click Connect to talk directly to the producer, or Order to place your order in their own shop. Thinking about a quarter, a half or a whole animal? Message them. They will tell you weights, price and when it will be ready.

Collection and delivery are both available. Delivery is by courier, in a chilled box, to your door, and the farm charges for it. A courier costs much the same to move one steak as a full box, so single cuts are usually collection.
