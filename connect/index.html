Producers you can message directly

The map pin is the farm gate. This is the conversation at it. Every producer here has opened a line to the people who eat their food. One tap and you are talking to the person who grew it, reared it or made it: what is ready this week, what is coming, and how to get it.

How it works

A producer joins Connect by giving Provenance one WhatsApp number. It becomes a Connect button on their map card and a place on this list, and nothing more. The number is never printed. The button opens the chat.

Your message goes straight to the farm. Nobody at Provenance reads it, and nobody stands between you and the person who made your food. That is the whole point.

Producing food in Ireland and want a line of your own? List your farm and ask for Connect.
