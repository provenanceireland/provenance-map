How it works

Each farm keeps a list of what they have. Not a shop and not a stock count: product names, updated when the year turns over or an animal goes. The date it was last touched is on every list, so you can see how current it is.

A quarter is roughly a freezer drawer, a half fills a chest freezer, a whole animal is usually split between households. The farm will tell you weights, price and hanging time. That conversation is theirs, not ours.
