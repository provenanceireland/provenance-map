How it works

Discover what's available. Each producer keeps their own list of what they currently have. Press See the farm for their profile, where the latest list sits alongside the date they sent it.

Connect directly. Press Connect to message the producer. You are talking to the person who grew or reared it, not to us.

Buy from the producer. Tell them what you want and organise collection or delivery between you. Provenance takes no part in the sale and receives no commission. Some farms also sell through their own online shop, and the link to it is on their profile.

Buying in bulk, or after something that is not on the list? A quarter, a half, a whole animal, a standing order? Message the producer and talk it through.

Farming in Ireland and want to be on this? List your farm and ask about Connect.
