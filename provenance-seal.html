The Provenance Seal

The label tells you what the law requires.
The Seal shows you the truth.

Every food product you buy carries a label. That label was designed by a legal team to satisfy a regulatory requirement. It tells you the country of origin, the weight and the ingredients in descending order.

It cannot tell you anything specific about the farm, the animal, the field or the person who grew it, and whether any of what it claims is actually true.

The Provenance Seal was built to answer every question the label cannot.

What the Seal Is

A QR code on a food product's packaging. Scan it to see the full Provenance.

When you scan it with your phone you arrive at a complete documentation page for that specific product from that specific production run. Not the brand's general farming claims. Not a marketing page. The specific batch. The specific source. The specific proof.

What the Seal Shows You

Every Provenance Seal page contains the same foundational elements regardless of the product it is attached to.

The named source

Not a country. Not a region. The specific farm, coastline, hive or production facility where this product came from. Named. Pinned on the Provenance Map, so you can see the farm location and visit footage on the illustrated Ireland map and explore every other verified producer nearby.

The documented process

How the product was made or harvested. In plain English. By the specific person who made it. No marketing language. No claims that cannot be verified.

The independent testing results

Selected Seal products are tested by an accredited independent laboratory. The results are published in full on the batch page. Not summarised. Not interpreted by the brand. Every marker tested. Every result shown. The legal limits displayed alongside each result so you can see not just that it passed but by exactly how much.

The chatbot

An AI assistant trained specifically on the documentation for that batch. Ask it anything. Which farm is this from? What does the testing show? Is this beef completely grass fed? When is the next batch available? It answers from verified data not from a script.

The Seal — Two Versions

V1 — Coming Soon

Batch-specific. Laboratory tested. AI-assisted.

The first iteration launches with Athnuachan Grass-Fed Beef Tallow from a local beef farm. The V1 Seal is batch-specific rather than producer-specific. Every production run has its own unique QR code pointing to its own unique batch documentation page.

The V2 batch page contains everything the V1 Seal carries and significantly more. Specific batch identity: the exact farm, the exact date, the exact jar count, the individual jar number out of the total batch produced. The full render process documented on film. Eurofins laboratory testing results published in full with EU legal limits shown alongside every marker. A chatbot trained specifically on the documentation for that batch.

Every batch is numbered, every batch is limited and when they are gone the batch page remains as a permanent record of something real that existed and sold out.

V2 — The Vision

On every shelf. In every shop.

The full Provenance vision is a consumer standing in a supermarket picking up a product, scanning the Seal with their phone, seeing the real farm behind it and then opening the map to discover every other verified real producer available nearby.

The V2 Seal is the Seal on products sold through conventional retail. Supermarket shelves, health food shops, butchers, delis, farm shops. The QR mark printed directly on the product label rather than applied as a sticker. The batch page loading in under two seconds on mobile data in a shop aisle. The map discovery layer showing every other Provenance-verified product within reach.

This is the Seal in its ultimate form. Not a niche product for conscious consumers who seek out verified food. A standard on any shelf, in any shop, available to any consumer who picks up a product and wants to know where it actually came from.

We are building toward it one batch at a time beginning in Wexford.
